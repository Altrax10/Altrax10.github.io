Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GabotClips Studio handles information when you use the Windows desktop application and its TikTok integration.

1. Information you provide

GabotClips Studio may process information you choose to provide or create through the application, including gaming video files, clip metadata, and information needed to connect your TikTok account.

2. TikTok account connection

When you connect TikTok, authentication and authorization are performed through TikTok's authorization flow. GabotClips Studio does not request or collect your TikTok password.

When permitted by the TikTok APIs and the permissions you authorize, the application may receive basic information about the connected TikTok account so the app can identify the connected account. Upload actions are initiated by you.

3. Video uploads

When you explicitly choose to upload a clip, the selected video is transmitted through the applicable TikTok API flow. TikTok may process the uploaded content according to TikTok's own privacy policies and terms.

4. Local data

Gaming clips and application settings may be stored locally on your Windows device. The exact local storage depends on the version and configuration of the application.

5. Data sharing

GabotClips Studio does not sell your personal information. Information is shared with TikTok only when necessary to perform a TikTok integration action that you initiate or authorize, and as required for the operation of the integration.

6. Data retention and deletion

You can remove locally stored clips and application data using the controls available on your device or in the application. If you disconnect a TikTok account, future authorized actions through that connection will stop, subject to the behavior of the TikTok service.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no software or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

GabotClips Studio is not directed at children. Do not use the application if you are not legally permitted to use the relevant services in your jurisdiction.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, integrations, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer through the GabotClips Support page or the Altrax10 GitHub profile.
