What GabotClips Studio does

The app is designed around a simple creator workflow: keep your clips organized, preview the right moment, and send selected content to your social workflow when you are ready.

Capture & manage

Keep gaming clips organized in a dedicated library so you can find the footage you want to use.

Preview & prepare

Preview and prepare selected clips before sharing them. The workflow is focused on creator-controlled uploads.

TikTok integration

Connect a TikTok account through TikTok's authorization flow and upload a selected video as a draft when supported by the connected account and TikTok API.

How the TikTok connection works

  1. You choose to connect your TikTok account from GabotClips Studio.
  2. TikTok handles authentication and authorization; GabotClips does not ask for your TikTok password.
  3. You explicitly select a clip and start the upload from the app.
  4. The TikTok integration is intended for creator-controlled sharing, with the uploaded video continuing through TikTok's normal creation workflow.
